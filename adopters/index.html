Independent products using SoulOS for persistent persona and memory. Not mutual endorsement.

SignalPR

AI-native PR execution platform for founders: real-time media event radar, newsjacking angle generation, journalist vector RAG matching, and outreach sync to Instantly, Smartlead, and Apollo.

SoulOS role: Persistent agent layer via hybrid sidecar — episodic memory, persona state (HEXACO MSV), and hybrid prepare/complete orchestration alongside AWS Bedrock and pgvector semantic search.

B2B SaaS, public relations, media monitoring, newsjacking

Aeterna

Interactive digital legacy platform: AI-guided life-story interviews, encrypted voice archives, family natural-language Q&A, and a digital twin that answers from the narrator's own recordings.

SoulOS role: Persistent persona and episodic memory so narrator identity, tone, and recalled life facts stay consistent across interview sessions and family conversations.

digital legacy, family technology, voice archives, digital twin

Ved Travel

Family Croatia travel expert: kid-friendly itineraries, hidden beaches, islands, national parks, and AI trip planning curated by Vedrana.

SoulOS role: Persistent travel-advisor persona and episodic session memory so multi-turn trip planning keeps family preferences, dates, and prior suggestions consistent.

travel, family travel, Croatia, AI trip planning

Getbyliner

AI press release distribution and journalist outreach for founders: match live reporter requests (Qwoted, HARO, and more), draft newsroom-ready releases, and distribute to press sites without an agency retainer.

SoulOS role: Persistent PR-agent persona and episodic memory for pitch context, company story, and multi-turn press-release drafting via the hybrid sidecar (prepare/complete) alongside the product’s LLM.

B2B SaaS, public relations, press releases, journalist outreach
